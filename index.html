Case study

Nature Stone landing page

Nature Stone Construction needed one page for people coming from Instagram and from partner introductions. The live page is the offer: hydraulic block machines and premium pavers, built for buyers in Nigeria.

The first screen states the work, then asks for a quotation. A quotation request is a lead, so it goes on the same list as every other partnership.

naturestonecontruction.com

  1. Offer first

    The headline is the product: precision block technology, with daily output of 12–13k blocks and a 15–25 second molding cycle.

  2. One next step

    Explore the machines, or get a quotation. Contact is a phone number, an email, and WhatsApp, not a second website.

  3. Proof on the page

    QT4-20, products, projects, properties, and hire sit on the same page, so a visitor can check the work before they ask.

  4. Same list as partnerships

    A Nature Stone quotation does not sit in a separate inbox. It is tagged with the source and given a next step.

How I track partnerships and leads

Every lead goes on one list the day it shows up. A DM, a comment, a referral, and a Nature Stone quotation all become the same kind of row.

  • Who they are, and where they came from.
  • What they asked for.
  • Status: new, talking, agreed, live, or passed.
  • The next step, and the date I will do it.

I review the list twice a week. A row with no next step is not being tracked. That is the whole system.
